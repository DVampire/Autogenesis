# Paper structure templates

Recommend a chapter structure automatically from the paper type; the user only confirms or tweaks it.

For the Chinese-language paper types, each entry gives the English name with the Chinese heading that goes into the manuscript in parentheses — use the Chinese form verbatim in the draft.

## Bachelor's thesis

```
1. Abstract (摘要)
2. Introduction (绪论)
   - Background and significance (研究背景与意义)
   - State of the field, domestic and international (国内外研究现状)
   - Scope and methods (研究内容与方法)
   - Structure of the thesis (论文结构安排)
3. Theoretical foundation / literature review (理论基础/文献综述)
4. Research design and methods (研究设计与方法)
5. Experiments and analysis / empirical study (实验与分析/实证研究)
6. Conclusion and outlook (结论与展望)
7. References (参考文献)
8. Acknowledgements (致谢)
```

**Length**: 8,000–15,000 characters

## Doctoral thesis

```
1. Abstract (摘要)
2. Introduction (绪论)
   - Background and statement of the problem (研究背景与问题提出)
   - Objectives and significance (研究目的与意义)
   - Methods and technical roadmap (研究方法与技术路线)
   - Novel contributions (论文创新点)
   - Structure of the thesis (论文结构)
3. Literature review (文献综述)
   - Theoretical foundation (理论基础)
   - State of the field and trends (研究现状与发展趋势)
   - Shortcomings of existing work (现有研究的不足)
4. Methodology (研究方法)
   - Research framework (研究框架)
   - Research design (研究设计)
   - Data sources and processing (数据来源与处理)
   - Analytical methods (分析方法)
5. Results (研究结果)
   - Descriptive analysis (描述性分析)
   - Main findings (主要发现)
6. Discussion (讨论)
   - Theoretical contribution (理论贡献)
   - Practical significance (实践意义)
   - Limitations (局限性)
7. Conclusion and future work (结论与未来研究方向)
8. References (参考文献)
9. Appendix (附录)
10. Acknowledgements (致谢)
```

**Length**: 50,000–100,000 characters

## How to use

1. Once the user confirms the paper type, load the matching structure automatically
2. When presenting it, ask whether anything needs adjusting
3. Common adjustments:
   - Add a case-study chapter
   - Merge or split chapters
   - Rename chapters
4. Record the confirmed structure in `plan/outline.md`
