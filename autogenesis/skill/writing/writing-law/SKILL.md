# Legal writing

Guidance specific to law papers. The conventions here target Chinese legal scholarship, so the citation formats, section-heading templates, and stock phrases stay in Chinese — they are the forms a Chinese law journal expects. The surrounding guidance is in English.

## 1. What makes law papers different

### Compared with other disciplines

| Aspect | Law papers | General humanities |
|--------|------------|--------------------|
| Core | Legal argument, doctrinal analysis | Argumentative logic |
| Citations | Statutes, judicial cases, doctrine | Academic literature |
| Structure | Problem-driven, doctrinal | Flexible |
| Language | Precise, terminologically strict | May be somewhat literary |

### Common types of law paper

- **Theoretical**: legal theory, philosophy of law
- **Doctrinal analysis**: statutory interpretation, institutional analysis
- **Case study**: analysis of a leading case, case commentary
- **Comparative law**: comparison with foreign legal systems
- **Empirical**: analysis of how the law works in practice
- **Legislative**: legislative proposals, institutional reform

## 2. Structure of a law paper

### Common structural patterns

**Problem-driven (most common)**
```
一、问题的提出            (statement of the problem)
二、现行法律规定及其不足   (current law and its shortcomings)
三、域外经验与比较借鉴     (foreign experience and comparative lessons)
四、完善建议              (proposals for reform)
五、结语                  (conclusion)
```

**Doctrinal analysis**
```
一、引言                        (introduction)
二、[制度/概念]的界定            (defining the institution/concept)
三、[制度/概念]的构成要件         (its constitutive elements)
四、[制度/概念]的法律效果         (its legal effects)
五、[制度/概念]的适用问题         (problems in application)
六、结论                        (conclusion)
```

**Case study**
```
一、案情简介与争议焦点   (facts and the issues in dispute)
二、裁判理由分析        (analysis of the court's reasoning)
三、学理评析            (doctrinal commentary)
四、制度反思与建议       (institutional reflection and proposals)
```

**Comparative law**
```
一、问题的提出                        (statement of the problem)
二、我国现行规定                      (current Chinese law)
三、域外立法例（德国法、日本法、英美法）  (foreign statutes: German, Japanese, common law)
四、比较与借鉴                        (comparison and lessons)
五、完善建议                          (proposals for reform)
```

## 4. Writing conventions

### Register

**Precision**
- Wrong: 这个法律规定不太合理 ("this rule isn't very reasonable")
- Right: 该条款在适用中存在以下问题 ("the provision raises the following problems in application")

**Formality**
- Wrong: 法院判错了 ("the court got it wrong")
- Right: 该判决在法律适用上存在值得商榷之处 ("the judgment's application of the law is open to question")

**Objectivity**
- Wrong: 我认为应该修改法律 ("I think the law should be amended")
- Right: 基于上述分析，现行规定有待完善 ("on the above analysis, the current rule requires refinement")

### Stock phrases

**Introducing a problem**
- 实践中存在...的问题 — in practice, the problem of … arises
- 学界对此存在争议 — scholarly opinion is divided
- 现行法对此规定不明 — current law is unclear on this point

**Analysis and argument**
- 从文义解释角度 — from the standpoint of literal interpretation
- 从体系解释角度 — from the standpoint of systematic interpretation
- 从目的解释角度 — from the standpoint of purposive interpretation
- 从历史解释角度 — from the standpoint of historical interpretation

**Comparative lessons**
- 域外立法例表明... — foreign legislation shows that …
- 比较法上的经验显示... — comparative experience indicates that …

**Concluding**
- 综上所述 — in summary
- 基于以上分析 — on the basis of the above analysis
- 由此可见 — it follows that

## 5. Research methods

### Doctrinal analysis

1. Identify the legal norm to be analyzed
2. Literal interpretation: the plain meaning
3. Systematic interpretation: its relation to other provisions
4. Purposive interpretation: the legislative purpose
5. Historical interpretation: the legislative background
6. Comparative interpretation: foreign provisions

### Case analysis

1. Facts: what was established
2. Issues: distill the legal questions
3. Judgment: the court's chain of reasoning
4. Commentary: theoretical support or criticism
5. Reflection: proposals for refining the rule

## 6. Law paper checklist

### Structure and logic
- [ ] The research problem is clearly identified
- [ ] The argument proceeds in clear layers
- [ ] The parts connect naturally
- [ ] The conclusion follows from the argument

### Citations
- [ ] Citation format is consistent
- [ ] Statutory citations are accurate
- [ ] Case citations are complete
- [ ] Foreign-language citations follow the convention

### Language
- [ ] Terminology is used accurately
- [ ] The register is objective and formal
- [ ] No colloquialisms

### Academic standards
- [ ] Every position has a source
- [ ] Citations are complete
- [ ] The novel contribution is explicit
